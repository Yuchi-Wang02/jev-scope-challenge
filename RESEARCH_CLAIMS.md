# Current research claims

This ledger summarizes the completed series through the
[payment-ownership Jev diagnostic](research/payment-ownership/RESULTS_V02.md).
It interprets saved evidence; it adds no experiment, approval or new research
commitment. Study-specific protocols, results and earlier claims retain their
historical scope. See the [research map](RESEARCH_INDEX.md) for execution status
and the [current novelty matrix](NOVELTY_MATRIX.md) for reuse boundaries.

**The repository supports narrow, inspectable diagnostics of decision pipelines.
It has not established whether a dedicated decision model is necessary.**

## What the completed evidence supports

The denominators below belong to different studies. Complete cases, paired
views, repeated option orders, lines and fields are not interchangeable or
independent samples. Follow each report for all controls, budgets and failures.

The gated method reuses 200 actual joint forwards; its projected 100-query
prefiltered subset was not separately executed or timed.

## What is not established

|Possible overstatement|Current evidence boundary|
|---|---|
|“A new decision architecture or extraction algorithm.”|The pointer architecture and adapted weights are upstream Kev artifacts. Native logits, order averaging, finite-state execution and ID equality are existing components. The current contribution is the diagnostic record and its controls; paper novelty remains unresolved.|
|“An ordinary unadapted small model replaces a dedicated decision model.”|Later N1 is Qwen3-4B-Base plus an already-trained historical Kev LoRA. No new training here does not mean no prior decision adaptation. Broad 2B/4B/8B comparisons and model necessity are unresolved.|
|“The gate transfers to natural language or unseen tasks.”|New scene instances retain inspected grammar, explicit IDs, one owner and one fact per line. Prepared rewrites, packed multi-fact variants and external-data review queues have no model results.|
|“The benchmark is independently validated.”|Two submissions cover the same 96 payment items, with a scope objection and process self-reports; no reference update is adjudicated. Program references validate the declared construction; they do not settle natural-language ambiguity. Public development inputs cannot become fresh confirmation by renaming a split.|
|“The pipeline is cheaper or deployment-ready.”|Calls, input tokens and selected timings are recorded, but gate CPU cost and full deployment/build/maintenance costs are not. No overall cost advantage or real-world error rate is established.|
|“The latest pass repairs the previous failed studies.”|Each screen belongs to its own frozen method and data. A subsequent pass or post-hoc improvement cannot retroactively turn a failed screen into a pass.|

## Evidence and release boundaries

N0 means unchanged Base/native; N1 means the same Base plus upstream Kev
LoRA/native; K1 uses the adapted backbone and historical Kev pointer path.
The original Qwen3-4B comparison used a different model artifact from later
Qwen3-4B-Base studies. These are pinned historical systems, not claims about
today's upstream releases. See [versions and attribution](THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md).

The earlier target-switch run contains 500 unique scientific forwards /188,797 input tokens
and two additional unscored warmups. Derived method rows and repeated views
do not increase its independent sample size. The
[frozen execution protocol](research/request-ownership/EXECUTION_PROTOCOL.md)
and [raw results](research/request-ownership/results/v0.1/) remain authoritative
for the run's configuration and resource ledger.

The [multi-fact reachability analysis](research/fact-execution/MULTIFACT_LINE_STRESS.md)
is a software/interface check, not a measured model result. The
[ShARC review preparation](research/external-validation/SHARC_REVIEW_PROTOCOL.md)
contains zero completed human reviews and zero model scores. Neither preparation
establishes language transfer, nor does either commit the project to a next study.

Offline recomputation and CI can establish consistency between saved evidence
and reports. They do not supply independent labels, a fresh inference
replication, a general reliability guarantee or a publication-ready contribution.
