# Nearest work and claim boundary

Bounded source review on 2026-09-30. The paper rows below use primary abstracts
and the previously registered sources, not independent replication or a complete
literature review. No performance numbers from those papers are reproduced here.

|Primary source|Already studied or supplied|What this diagnostic can add / cannot claim|
|---|---|---|
|[tau2-bench source](https://github.com/sierra-research/tau2-bench/tree/5bfa7e37b36656b37dc6d022156be6563c1007f3)|Public simulated retail policy, records and tools.|A small static payment-destination component adaptation. Not a new service environment, real-world dataset, or tau success score.|
|[How do Language Models Bind Entities in Context?](https://arxiv.org/abs/2310.17191)|Entity-attribute binding and internal causal analysis.|Object binding is not a new problem. This study has no activation intervention or internal-mechanism evidence.|
|[Legible Failures](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.11216)|Entity-obligation binding, query-entity counterfactuals, trained probes and steering.|Target switching is not novel. Our external component check neither reproduces nor supersedes their learned intervention.|
|[When History Lies](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.06057)|Misleading tool-use history; paired input conditions including oracle state; policy transfer.|Our full/related comparison is another bounded diagnostic, not an original oracle-control concept or a multi-turn evaluation.|
|[CheckList](https://aclanthology.org/2020.acl-main.442/) and [Contrast Sets](https://aclanthology.org/2020.findings-emnlp.117/)|Behavioral tests and controlled local changes.|Pairs and invariance checks are reused evaluation ideas, not invented here.|

Potential contribution is an independently auditable boundary result for a specific
typed decision system under a sourced rule, with fair direct controls and retained
negative outcomes. It remains an exploratory replication/engineering diagnostic.
Two reviewed mappings, structured JSON and deterministic rules are not algorithms
we invented. A strong ordinary-model comparator and fresh independent evaluation
would be needed for broader model comparisons. A new benchmark name alone does not
create research novelty.
