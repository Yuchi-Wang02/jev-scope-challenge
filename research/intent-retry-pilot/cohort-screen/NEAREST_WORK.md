# Nearest-work boundary for the continuation idea

Read on 2026-10-05 local / 2026-10-06 UTC. This is a bounded novelty check, not an exhaustive review or a replication.

- [Beyond Task Completion: Training Capable and Safe Computer-Use Agents / SCOPE](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.22178v1) already studies continuing safely when a legitimate path remains, alongside refusal and ordinary completion, using paired risk tasks and post-training. Its aggregate benchmark score is not a direct measurement of successful safe completion on every risky task. Therefore “continue the safe part instead of refusing everything” alone is not our new idea.
- [Coding Agents Are Guessing / UnderSpecBench](https://arxiv.org/html/2607.02294v1) already examines underspecified coding decisions, distinguishes asking, refusing and deferring, and measures wrong-target, over-scope and safe-success outcomes. Merely adding action-boundary labels or clarification choices would not establish a separate contribution.

Neither source was run or reproduced here. Their existence does not show that the specific state/receipt defect found in AgentAbstain has already been reported. Conversely, that one software defect does not establish a novel model method, a general benchmark failure rate, or the value of a small critic.

Any renewed scientific proposal needs a narrower unestablished question, a program baseline, a capable ordinary-model comparison, new independently reviewed material, and a measurable improvement or boundary. This screen does not supply those results.
