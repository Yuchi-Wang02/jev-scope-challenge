# Gates before the next study

1. Are all missing-evidence policies and natural-language renderings independently
   judged unambiguous? Current labels are AI-checked; provide a reviewer sheet and
   log objections without editing cases to favor a model.
2. Does the native/pointer gap persist across predefined equivalent layouts,
   distinct source documents and genuinely new relation combinations? The current
   test holds out styles/entities within known construction families only.
3. Does it persist with current validated software and alternate hardware/dtypes?
   The historical port and one-input kernel check are insufficient for a universal
   implementation claim.
4. What remains after ordinary prompt/serialization controls? Only then decide
   whether a new head/LoRA training experiment is the right intervention.
5. For H0/H1, which fresh labeled corpus and grouped splits, equal tuning budgets,
   seeds, total compute limits and meaningful effect sizes will be frozen?
6. Which subset is an executable software policy? C0 reads construction facts and
   does not establish coverage over arbitrary real language.

Do not use these inspected pilot cases as untouched confirmation. The next stage
needs a new registered protocol; head-only learning is supervised training.
