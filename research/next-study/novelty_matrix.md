# Novelty boundary

|Component|Existing precedent|Our present contribution|
|---|---|---|
|Native versus adapted LM head|Kev's public probe|Exact pinned replication path and new synthetic diagnostic records|
|Pointer head / dynamic candidates|Kev, Laya and other classifiers|Attributed reuse; no architecture claim|
|Hold/flip/removed-evidence tests|CheckList, contrast sets, upstream diagnostics|Original controlled examples, not new evaluation theory|
|Temperature fitting / confidence selection|Standard calibration; upstream metrics|Fixed calibration/test separation and inspectable small-sample limits|
|Math-kernel parity control|Known numerical/backend issue class|Documented local engineering failure and resolution|
|Frozen head-only versus joint training|Planned, not executed|No contribution yet; requires matched new training study|

The current release is a reproducible diagnostic/engineering contribution.
Paper novelty remains unresolved and must come from new mechanisms or sufficiently
informative broad empirical evidence, with independent labeling and strong controls.
